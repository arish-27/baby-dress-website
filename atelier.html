Atelier Quality Assurance

Our 21-Point Infant Safety Protocol

Every single frock passes through 21 safety check stations in our South Hub Atelier before being packaged in breathable muslin gift wraps.

1

Pre-Wash Sanitization

Garments undergo gentle steam washing at 60°C to eliminate mill residues and dust before delivery.

2

Dual Needle Scan

Each piece passes through a digital metal detector to guarantee zero broken sewing needles or metal fragments.

3

15kg Snap Pull Test

Diaper snap buttons are stress-tested to 150 Newtons of force so they never detach or present choking hazards.

4

Non-Toxic Dyes Test

Every fabric dye lot is independently lab-verified free of formaldehyde, phthalates, and heavy metals.

5

Hypoallergenic Seam Check

Internal seams are finger-tested by infant apparel specialists to ensure zero rough ridges or protruding threads.

6

Breathability Airflow Test

Muslin weaves are tested for optimal thermal regulation to prevent infant overheating and sweat rashes.
